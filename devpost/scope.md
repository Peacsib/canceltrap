# CancelTrap

AI-powered dark-pattern subscription defuser & trial kill-switch to cancel subscriptions before you get charged.

## The Unique Kernel
The moment Gemini analyzes an uploaded subscription screenshot or receipt, pinpoints the renewal deadline and sneaky dark retention pattern, and instantly equips the user with a step-by-step defusal checklist, a direct portal bypass button, and a 1-click calendar kill-switch (.ics) alongside the 5-year "Vampire Bleed" calculation to stop the recurring charge before the trap springs.

## Who It's For
Developers, tech professionals, and students who frequently sign up for modern developer tools, AI platforms, and creative SaaS (like Lovable, Adobe, or workout apps) and risk getting hit by sneaky auto-renewals, hidden cancellation buttons, or confusing downgrade mazes. Today, they scramble through nested settings menus, forget renewal dates, or give up and eat unwanted charges.

## The Core Loop
1. Upload a billing receipt, subscription page screenshot, or trial welcome email (or select one of 3 realistic demo presets: Lovable Pro, Adobe Annual, or 7-Day AI Trial).
2. Gemini extracts merchant details, recurring price, trial/renewal deadline, and specific dark pattern tricks.
3. Review the actionable "Defusal Dossier":
   - **Renewal Deadline & Countdown**: Exactly how much time remains before the charge hits.
   - **5-Year Vampire Bleed**: Compounding financial projection of what this subscription will drain if not cancelled in time.
   - **Step-by-Step Dark-Pattern Defusal Checklist**: Practical breakdown exposing decoy buttons, "Pause" traps, or multi-page mazes.
   - **Direct Portal Bypass Button**: Instant direct URL to the merchant's hidden cancellation/billing page.
   - **1-Click Calendar Kill-Switch (.ics)**: Pre-configured calendar reminder set 24–48 hours prior to renewal so the user never forgets.

## Inspiration & Identity

- Modern enterprise dashboard feel with translucent glass cards, soft atmospheric blue gradients, clean frosted upload panels, and sharp typography.
- Avoids aggressive neon cyberpunk tropes in favor of calm, authoritative institutional confidence.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
A university student and full-stack developer committed to practicing a disciplined, plan-first workflow with AI agents, focusing on defining a rock-solid multimodal Gemini JSON contract and edge-case handling before touching UI code.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A 60-second end-to-end screen demonstration where:
1. A user drops a receipt screenshot or clicks a preset (e.g., "Adobe Annual Early-Fee Trap" or "Lovable Pro $20/mo Trap").
2. Gemini 2.5 Flash analyzes the image, identifies the merchant, the upcoming renewal deadline, the financial bleed, and the exact dark pattern.
3. The user follows the step-by-step defusal checklist, clicks the direct cancellation portal link, and clicks "Download Kill-Switch" to generate an actionable .ics calendar event alert.

## The POC Boundary
- **In Now**:
  - Image upload (drag-and-drop / file picker) + 3 interactive demo presets for instant testing.
  - Multimodal Gemini 2.5 Flash integration with strict structured JSON output schema.
  - Actionable Defusal Dossier:
    - Renewal deadline display & countdown alert.
    - 5-Year Vampire Bleed compounding calculation widget.
    - Dark-pattern breakdown + step-by-step defusal instructions.
    - Curated direct cancellation portal button.
    - 1-Click calendar reminder generator (.ics download) configured 24-48h prior to the renewal date.
  - Premium glassy blue responsive interface.
- **Later**:
  - Browser extension to auto-detect cancel mazes live.
  - Client-side OCR fallback.
  - Direct inbox sync (Google/Outlook calendar/email integration).
- **Explicitly Cut**:
  - **Post-charge refund letters, FTC dispute tones, and chargeback claims**: Cut to focus 100% on proactive timely cancellation before money leaves the bank.
  - **User accounts and persistent databases**: Cut to keep the proof of concept 100% stateless and zero-friction.
  - **Backend automated email sending (SMTP)**: Cut in favor of standalone client-side tools and calendar files.
  - **Live web scraping/crawling of merchant pages**: Cut due to anti-bot measures; replaced by curated direct links and Gemini knowledge.
